Crank Nicolson Method for Solving Parabolic Partial Differential Equations

S. E. Fadugba, O. H. Edogbanya, and S. C. Zelibe

International Journal of Applied Mathematics and Modeling, 1(3), 8–23

Presents the numerical formulation and error analysis of the Crank-Nicolson finite difference scheme for parabolic partial differential equations. The work proves unconditional numerical stability and establishes rapid convergence for initial-boundary value problems.

R. B. Ogunrinde, S. E. Fadugba, and J. T. Okunlola

Mathematical Theory and Modeling, 2(8), 25–34

A comparative evaluation of classical single-step and multistep numerical integration schemes (Euler, Improved Euler, and Runge-Kutta) for solving initial value problems, detailing computational stability regions and discretization errors.

Dynamic Model of COVID-19 and Citizens Reaction Using Fractional Derivative

R. B. Ogunrinde, U. K. Nwajeri, S. E. Fadugba, R. R. Ogunrinde, and K. I. Oshinubi

Alexandria Engineering Journal, 60(2), 2001–2012 (Elsevier)

Formulates a non-linear epidemiological transmission model using Caputo fractional-order derivatives to capture memory effects and public behavioral reactions during viral epidemics. Stability and numerical simulations demonstrate intervention efficacy.

S. E. Fadugba

Chaos, Solitons & Fractals, 138, 109968 (Elsevier)

Derives analytical and semi-analytical series solutions for European call options governed by the time-fractional Black-Scholes partial differential equation via the Homotopy Analysis Method (HAM), providing exact closed-form convergence proofs.
