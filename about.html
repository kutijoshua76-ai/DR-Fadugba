Executive Profile

Bridging Mathematical Rigor with Institutional Governance

Applied Mathematician • Institutional Leader • Data Governance Expert

Dr. Sunday Emmanuel FADUGBA, PhD is a prominent academic, researcher, and university administrator at Ekiti State University, Ado Ekiti. With nearly two decades of commitment to higher education, computational research, and academic leadership, Dr. Fadugba combines profound scientific expertise in Applied and Financial Mathematics with strategic administrative roles that guide university research, data privacy compliance, and foundational undergraduate pedagogy.

Presently, Dr. Fadugba serves as the Deputy Director of the Office of Research, Development and Innovation at Ekiti State University, driving institutional capacity for scholarly discovery, competitive research grants, commercialization, and interdisciplinary collaboration. Simultaneously, he acts as the university's statutory Data Protection Officer (DPO), instituting robust data governance, digital privacy frameworks, and regulatory compliance protocols across all faculties and administrative divisions.

Prior to his current portfolio, Dr. Fadugba held critical leadership mandates within the university's Directorate of General Studies, having served as Deputy Director (2025–2026) and subsequently as Coordinator (January – July 2026). In these capacities, he steered large-scale curriculum coordination, standard quality assurance, and instructional modernization for thousands of undergraduate scholars across diverse academic disciplines.
