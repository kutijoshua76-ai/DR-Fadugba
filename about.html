Leadership & Governance — Administrative Roles

Leadership & Governance

Dr. Fadugba currently serves as Deputy Director of the Office of Research, Development and Innovation, driving institutional capacity for scholarly discovery, competitive research grants, commercialization, and interdisciplinary collaboration. Simultaneously, as the university's statutory Data Protection Officer (DPO), he institutes robust data governance, digital privacy frameworks, and regulatory compliance across all faculties.

Prior to these roles, he held critical leadership mandates within the Directorate of General Studies, serving as Deputy Director (2025–2026) and Coordinator (January–July 2026), steering large-scale curriculum coordination and quality assurance for thousands of undergraduate scholars.
