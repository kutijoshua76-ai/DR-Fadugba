Financial Mathematics & Calculus — Research Pillars

01

Financial Mathematics & Derivative Valuation

Developing analytical Mellin transform representations and numerical PDE discretizations for option pricing under jump-diffusion and fractional Brownian motion models. Rigorous stability and convergence proofs ground every proposed scheme.

Fractional Calculus & Solitary Wave Solutions

Investigating non-linear fractional differential operators with memory effects via the Adomian Decomposition Method and homotopy perturbation schemes to derive exact soliton families.

Fractional PDEsAdomian MethodSolitons

Numerical Methods & Quantitative Risk — Research Pillars

02

High-Order Numerical Schemes for SDEs & PDEs

Constructing unconditionally stable Runge-Kutta and finite difference methods for stiff non-linear stochastic systems, establishing rigorous convergence bounds and consistency order.

Numerical AnalysisFinite DifferenceError Analysis
06

Quantitative Risk Assessment for Emerging Markets

Applying quantitative scenario simulations, Value-at-Risk (VaR) techniques, and econometric forecasting models to sovereign debt and foreign exchange instabilities.

EconometricsRisk AssessmentMarket Volatility

03

Mathematical Epidemiology & Population Dynamics
