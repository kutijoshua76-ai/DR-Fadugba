Key Pillars & Inquiries

Research Domains

Exploring fundamental and applied boundaries across numerical computation, stochastic calculus, and mathematical modeling.

01

Financial Mathematics & Derivative Valuation

Developing analytical Mellin transform representations and numerical PDE discretizations for option pricing under jump-diffusion and fractional Brownian motion models.

Black-Scholes Models Mellin Transform Stochastic Volatility
02

High-Order Numerical Schemes for SDEs & PDEs

Constructing unconditionally stable Runge-Kutta and finite difference methods for stiff non-linear stochastic systems, establishing rigorous convergence bounds and consistency order.

Numerical Analysis Finite Difference Error Analysis
03

Mathematical Epidemiology & Population Dynamics

Formulating deterministic and stochastic compartmental models (SEIR/SIRS) with non-linear incidence rates, optimal control protocols, and parameter estimation from clinical surveillance.

Epidemic Models Optimal Control Stability Analysis
04

Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)

Integrating deep neural architectures with physical conservation laws and differential equations to solve inverse boundary value problems with sparse observational datasets.

Scientific ML Deep Learning Inverse Problems
05

Fractional Calculus & Solitary Wave Solutions

Investigating non-linear fractional differential operators with memory effects via the Adomian Decomposition Method and homotopy perturbation schemes.

Fractional PDEs Adomian Method Solitons
06

Quantitative Risk Assessment for Emerging Markets

Applying quantitative scenario simulations, Value-at-Risk (VaR) techniques, and econometric forecasting models to sovereign debt and foreign exchange instabilities.

Econometrics Risk Assessment Market Volatility
